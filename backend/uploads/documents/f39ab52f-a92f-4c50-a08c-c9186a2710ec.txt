Company Remote Work Policy

Overview
This policy outlines the guidelines for employees who work remotely, either full-time or on a hybrid schedule.

Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period and hold a role that does not require physical presence on-site are eligible to apply for remote work arrangements. Approval is granted by the employee's direct manager.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time home office stipend of $300 to remote employees. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and reliable internet connection.

Working Hours
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10 AM to 3 PM in their local time zone, and to attend all scheduled team meetings via video call.

Security
All remote work must be performed using company-issued devices with full-disk encryption and VPN access enabled. Employees may not access company systems from public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without using the VPN.
